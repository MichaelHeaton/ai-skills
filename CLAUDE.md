## Branch and PR rules

**Never push directly to `main`.** All changes go through a pull request.

- Branch: `feat/...`, `fix/...`, or `chore/...`
- Open PR with `gh pr create` — do not merge; owner reviews
- No force-push without explicit instruction

## Content caution

Public repo. No employer names, Jira keys, or internal URLs in commits. Placeholder definitions will live under `categories/` when added.

## Skills (current)
