# [work-primary] — 3P update (public stub)

3P = Progress, Plans, Problems. Weekly team status for a **work-primary** domain context.

**Audience:** Leadership and adjacent teams
**Tone:** Factual, concise, skimmable in under one minute

## What to gather

- **Progress** — what shipped or closed this period
- **Plans** — top priorities next period
- **Problems** — blockers with owner or ask

Use Jira/Slack from `local.json` when available. Prefer **private examples** (personal KB repo) for your organization's naming and channels.

## Format

```
**[Team Name] — [Date Range]**

**Progress**
- [Outcome-focused bullet]

**Plans**
- [Priority]

**Problems**
- [Blocker] — [owner or ask]
```

If `Problems` is empty, write "None this week."
