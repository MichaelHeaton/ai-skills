# [work-primary] — Customer notification (public stub)

External or customer-facing notification after an event or change.

**Tone:** Empathetic, clear, action-oriented

## What to gather

- What happened, customer impact, current status, what customers should do, support contact, next update timing

## Format

```
**Subject:** [clear, non-alarmist]

[Opening — acknowledge impact]

**What happened:** [brief]
**Current status:** [resolved / ongoing]
**What you need to do:** [steps or "no action required"]

**Next update:** [when or channel]
```
